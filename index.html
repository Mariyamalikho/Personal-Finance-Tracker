Privacy Policy

Your Data is 100% Local and Private.

This Personal Finance Tracker is designed with a privacy-first approach. Here is what you need to know:

  • No Servers: We do not use external servers or databases to store your information.
  • Local Storage: All transactions and login details are saved securely in your web browser's Local Storage. Your data never leaves your device.
  • Security Notice: Because this is a portfolio demonstration app that stores data locally in your browser, please do not use your real banking passwords or highly sensitive credentials to log in.

By using this application, you acknowledge that you are responsible for the security of your own device.
